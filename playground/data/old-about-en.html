Mission

Develop Leaders.
Transform the Future.

Develop leaders, drive the future

HRI DeOne is committed to developing leaders who not only grow in their own capabilities, but are ready to create positive change for people, organizations, and society, helping drive a better future together.

Vision

A trusted partner
in shaping organizations
that thrive across generations.

The partner organizations trust
to build organizations that grow sustainably from generation to generation

HRI DeOne aims to be more than a training provider. We stand beside organizations as a long-term partner, developing people, leaders, and organizations to be strong, adapt sustainably in the business world, and pass on value continuously from generation to generation.

Core Values

Where Differences Become Strength.

When differences become strength

HRI DeOne was born from combining the strengths of two organizations. We believe
the differences in people, experiences, and perspectives become a strength that helps us learn, grow, and create greater value together.

Message from Our Leadership

LEADER MESSAGE

The founding of HRI DeOne
is not simply the merger of two companies.
It is the union of two strengths,
two cultures, and one shared vision.

HR Institute is a leading leadership and human resource development organization that has been trusted by leading Japanese companies since 1993, while DeOne Academy brings expertise in Leadership and Transformation along with a deep understanding of Thai organizations.

By combining the strengths of both organizations, we are confident we can create value greater than either could achieve alone. This is an important step in elevating leadership and organizational development in Thailand and connecting it with international standards.

The business world is changing rapidly with AI, technology, and new challenges. Organizations no longer need just training programs. They need a partner who can develop leaders, drive change, and elevate the capabilities of the entire organization in concrete ways. This is the role HRI DeOne is committed to playing.

Dr. Chaipat  Lertlucktaweekul

Board of Director, CEO
HRI DeOne Co., Ltd.

HRI DeOne aims to be
a bridge between Japan, Thailand,
and Southeast Asia,
and a partner in building
future growth together with all of you.

Over the past 30 years, we have continuously developed and accumulated expertise in management, human resource development, and organizational development alongside Japanese companies. We firmly believe this knowledge can cross borders and create real value for organizations in Thailand and across Southeast Asia.

We are committed to developing services and development approaches elevated by the combined strengths of both organizations, and to delivering that value to our clients in the best possible way.

We are deeply proud to begin this new chapter together with our colleagues and partners, and we are full of anticipation for the future ahead. I sincerely hope you will support us and join HRI DeOne on this new journey of growth.

Mr. Ken Misaka

Board of Director, Chairman
